PROJECT GALLERY

Haworth Industrial Technologies has facilitated the mechanical design and production aspect of business ventures of various scales. Services include providing professional mechanical design drafts of potential products,  expediting sourcing and prototype development by means of local and overseas manufacturers, and technical assistance in enhancing product functionality. Below is a small sample and descriptions of projects that have been developed through Haworth Industrial Technologies.

SOUTHLAND SENSING LLC

Southland Sensing is a manufacturer specializing in the design and development of analytical sensors and instrumentation. Southland Sensing offers a full range of stock products and also specializes in custom designed sensors, sample systems, packaging and electronic solutions.

http://www.sso2.com/

HYON USA

Haworth Industrial Technologies has developed the CUETÉ (Spanish- “FireWork”) RDA system for Hyon USA. After receiving conceptual sketches from HYON USA, Haworth Industrial Technologies provided the mechanical engineering design, 3D printed prototypes, and recommendations regarding means of production to make this product into reality.

Product Description:
RDA is an acronym for “Rebuildable Drip Atomizer” and refers to the design allowing for the replacement of the coils. The CUETÉ  RDA system is crafted from 303 Stainless Steel and has a 25 mm diameter. The two post arrangement with grub screws were designed to ensure secure connection as well replaceability of the coil.

https://www.instagram.com/hyonmods/?hl=en

500  STONES

Haworth Industrial Technologies helped develop the BARE BACK RACK for 500 STONES. The simple, effective design allows installation in 3 minutes as it only involves the assembly of 8 parts. Haworth Industrial Technologies was responsible for the design validation and prototype development. The prototype development was accomplished through use of local vendors for sourcing fabrication.
 
Product Description:
The BARE BACK RACK consists of two sets of 58” steel crossbars that are spaced 32” apart to accommodate for large cargo. This product is designed to fit all 2007-2017 Jeep Wranglers.

Coolit Baskit

Haworth Industrial Technologies was tasked with developing a solution for an inventor who wanted to create a means of cooling fast food that was too hot for their children to touch right away. The inventor of the Coolit Baskit provided a rough concept drawing that we were able to convert to a 3D model, then a 3D printed prototype, and eventually a finished manufacturing drawing.

Haworth Industrial Technologies was also able to provide the customer a source to manufacture the product affordably.
