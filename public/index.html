Frequently Asked Questions

What CAD software does Haworth Industrial Technologies use? ↓

We specialize in Dassault Systèmes SolidWorks for 3D parametric part design, complex assembly modeling, and engineering production drawings. We can also import and deliver neutral CAD formats including STEP, IGES, Parasolid (.x_t), and DXF/DWG.

Can Haworth Industrial Technologies help with prototyping and physical manufacturing? ↓

Yes. Beyond CAD design, we produce 3D printed prototypes to validate mechanics and interface directly with vetted local Southern California machine shops and overseas partners for CNC machining, sheet metal fabrication, and production injection molding.

What is your contracting structure? ↓

We operate on a flexible contracting model. Depending on your needs, we support fixed-price milestone projects, hourly engineering design consulting, or embedded contract engineering support for fast-moving product development sprints.
